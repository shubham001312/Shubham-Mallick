Who I am

I’m not learning AI only from a theoretical angle — I’m building toward real engineering ability.

My focus is backend systems, APIs, databases, machine learning, LLM applications, RAG, agents, and deployment. The goal is clear: become a strong backend engineer for AI products first, then grow into AI Platform / ML Infrastructure engineering.

I care about code that is observable, testable, and shipped — not just notebooks. Most of what I ship is open source so the work speaks for itself.

ImuTechz is the open-source research & development organisation I founded — the Agents & Skills directory of 10,631 open-source entries, IMURUN (local-first AI on its own C++ engine, FISH), and the website that ships them.

Today I’m an AI Developer intern at Potion Labs AI Technologies in Kolkata and an AI intern at Autom8x / SWOT Management (US). Shipped, not just built.
